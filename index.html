The Story

Classic American barbering tradition brought to one of Portugal's most beautiful fishing villages. We're not just a barbershop - we're a cultural collision.

Located in Cabanas de Tavira, a sun-soaked village on the edge of the Ria Formosa lagoon, where traditional fishing boats bob in turquoise waters and terracotta-roofed homes bake in the Algarve heat.

We bring timeless American barbershop craftsmanship - precision fades, hot towel shaves, classic pompadours - to locals and visitors looking for quality cuts in paradise.
